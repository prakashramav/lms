# AI Career Copilot & Interview Intelligence

## 1. Overview
The Career Copilot bridges student learning outcomes with industry recruitment. It operates as an evidence-driven advisory system analyzing verified platform achievements (course completions, coding problem solutions, and capstone projects) against real job market requirements.

---

## 2. Core Modules

### A. Skill Gap & Learning Path Engine
- Compares a student's active **Knowledge Profile** against targeted job requirements.
- Generates structured gap breakdowns:
  - **Matched Skills**: Confirmed with assessment scores and project tags.
  - **Developing Skills**: Currently enrolled in related curriculum modules.
  - **Missing Skills**: Recommends targeted courses and practice challenges.

### B. ATS Resume Optimization
- Formats resume sections (Experience, Projects, Education, Verified Skills) according to industry standard ATS schemas.
- Compares resume text against target job descriptions to highlight missing keywords.
- Strict constraint: **Never invents unearned credentials or phantom job experiences.**

### C. Simulated Mock Interview Engine
- Supports multiple tracks: Frontend, Backend, Fullstack, Machine Learning, and System Design.
- Progression:
  1. AI presents role-specific scenario question.
  2. Student submits technical or behavioral response.
  3. AI evaluates relevance, technical accuracy, and structural clarity.
  4. AI returns constructive improvement tips and an example of a stronger response before advancing.
- Transparency notice: All interview feedback is advisory and does not guarantee employment.
